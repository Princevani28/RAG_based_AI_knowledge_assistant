# Acme Corp HR Policy

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per year. Employees can carry over up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Parental leave is 16 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
