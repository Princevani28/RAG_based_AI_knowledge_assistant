# Acme Corp IT Security Standard

## Passwords
The minimum password length is 14 characters, and passwords must be rotated every 90 days. Password reuse across systems is prohibited.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate systems, including email and VPN.

## Phishing
Suspected phishing emails must be reported to security@acme-corp.example within 1 hour of receipt. Do not click links in suspicious emails.

## Devices
All company laptops use full-disk encryption. A VPN is required whenever connecting from public Wi-Fi networks.
