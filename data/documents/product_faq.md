# Acme Insight Product FAQ

## Pricing
The Starter plan costs $29 per month and supports up to 5 users. The Pro plan costs $99 per month and supports up to 25 users. Enterprise pricing is custom.

## API Limits
The Pro plan API rate limit is 100 requests per minute. Starter plan customers get 20 requests per minute.

## Data Retention
Customer analytics data is retained for 13 months, after which it is automatically deleted.

## Support
Enterprise customers receive a 1 hour first-response support SLA. Pro customers receive next business day support.
